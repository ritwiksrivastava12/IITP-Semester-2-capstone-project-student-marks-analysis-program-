How to read your reports

One consistent grading model

SGPA

Credit-weighted grade points for one semester. If marks are missing, the displayed SGPA is provisional and uses only graded subjects.

CGPA

Credit-weighted grade points across complete semesters only. A semester with any pending mark is excluded until it is complete.

Grade scale

O 10 · A+ 9 · A 8 · B+ 7 · B 6 · C 5 · F 0. A subject passes at 40% or above.

Charts

Charts A–E are rendered on demand and never cached. Their numbers come from the same transcript snapshot as the pages.
